- Production: Aurelian Parallel E2E Certification 20260903-B
- Canonical concept: Symphony of Serenity

- Toolchain: Remotion 4.0.506, React 19.2.0, deterministic React/CSS markup
- Frame contract: 720 frames at 24 fps (30.0 seconds)

The Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts deliverables are immutable copies of the same AurelianVertical render only when their checksums confirm byte identity. Technical observations, timestamps, render settings, checksums, and exact derivation are recorded only after successful rendering.

The 16:9 master is rendered from AurelianMaster at 1920x1080 and upscaled exactly 2x to the required 3840x2160 using the pinned Remotion FFmpeg binary and Lanczos scaling. This is a resolution-compliant programmatic upscale, not a claim of native 4K source detail.
