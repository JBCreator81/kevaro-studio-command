# Aurelian final-content clearance and independent QA protocol

Production: **Aurelian Parallel E2E Certification 20260903-B**
Canonical concept: **Symphony of Serenity**
Current disposition: **CLEARANCE PASS — INDEPENDENT QA PASS — READY FOR DELIVERY**

A checklist is not a pass. Record observed values, evidence references, reviewer identity, timestamp, and disposition only against each immutable media file.

## Per-deliverable technical verification

- File opens and decodes from beginning to end without error.
- SHA-256 matches the governed asset storage checksum.
- Duration is 30 seconds within the approved export tolerance documented by the operator.
- Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts are 9:16; the master is 16:9.
- Resolution meets the manifest target; 4K is used where practical for the master.
- Video is MP4/H.264 at 24 fps, with no broken frames or unexpected frame-rate conversion.
- Audio is present, audible, synchronized, unclipped, and derived only from the registered original score and SFX.
- Playback is verified on at least one standards-compliant desktop player; platform-upload validation is recorded where access exists.

## Per-deliverable content verification

- Content consistently presents **Symphony of Serenity**; `Everyday Glow` does not appear.
- Any depicted or named prop is **Aurelian Renewal Serum**, remains fictional, and is not associated with a real manufacturer or product.
- No identifiable real talent, recognizable real location, external recording, sample, stock/library SFX, or unregistered font appears.
- No diagnosis, treatment, cure, prevention, guaranteed outcome, scientific efficacy, or other unsupported medical claim appears.
- On-screen copy is spelled correctly and remains legible.
- Spoken-word captions are accurate, synchronized, and complete. If there is no speech, record `NOT APPLICABLE — NO SPOKEN WORDS`; do not invent captions.
- Essential text and visual information remain inside platform-safe areas throughout playback.
- Cuts, transitions, grade, typography, motion, and audio maintain intentional continuity.

## Separation and gates

1. The editor/asset creator records export and probe evidence but cannot be the sole verifier.
2. Clearance/Compliance reviews claims, brand/prop use, sourcing, and rights evidence.
3. An independently assigned QA reviewer verifies the immutable cleared files and records PASS or FAIL with findings.
4. Readiness is recalculated only after current clearance and QA records exist.
5. Studio Head final approval and finalization occur only after every required deliverable passes.
