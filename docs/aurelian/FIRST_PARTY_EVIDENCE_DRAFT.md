## Proposed delivery and upload workflow

This is the production handoff method, not access to client social accounts. Kevaro does not require or store Instagram or YouTube credentials for clearance.

1. Produce a 24 fps source master and derive validated delivery files.
2. Package: Instagram Reels 9:16, YouTube Shorts 9:16, and 16:9 web/social master; include captions, checksums, clearance manifest, and production notes.
3. Run technical QA for duration, aspect ratio, resolution, captions/safe zones, audio, playback, filenames, and checksums.
4. Place the approved package in the governed delivery location and record the immutable storage reference/version.
5. A client-designated upload operator downloads the package and uploads through client-controlled native or approved publishing tools. Credentials stay outside Kevaro.
6. Client-side publication confirmation is a delivery receipt, not a prerequisite for production-media clearance.

## Rights-avoiding production sourcing policy

- **Talent:** use no identifiable real person. Use abstract detail shots, non-identifiable generated forms, objects, textures, and environments. If a real person is introduced, obtain a release and reopen the talent gate.
- **Locations:** use wholly generated/constructed environments with retained provenance and no identifiable real property. Any real location requires an owner/location release.
- **Music:** create an original score specifically for this production and retain generation/composition provenance. No commercial recording, sample, or unverified model input is permitted.
- **Fonts:** proposed family is Noto Sans. Google states Google Fonts are open-source and usable commercially; the official Noto repository identifies OFL-1.1. Preserve the exact font file/version and OFL text in the asset manifest. Sources: https://developers.google.com/fonts and https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts.
- **SFX:** record or generate original ambient sound specifically for the production and retain source/generation provenance. Do not use an external library without its licence record.

This policy eliminates a need to acquire third-party assets; it does not clear assets that have not yet been created and verified.
